# Collapsible Groups 2.0.0-beta1 — Minecraft 1.20.1

- You can now use Collapsible Groups with EMI on Fabric and Forge.
- Updated JEI 15 compatibility while keeping support for older builds.
- Organize groups into your own categories, and rename categories locally.
- Built-in categories for mods you haven't installed stay out of the manager.
- All loaders now share the same settings screen.
- Searchable lists make it easier to find ingredients, tags, and mods when editing rules.
- Less waiting after editing a group, with your scroll position kept when you return to the manager.
- Empty groups are hidden by default. Turn on **Show empty groups** in **Sort and filter** to see them.
- Resource packs can add groups and translate their names.
- Fixed several rule-editing, preview, and tooltip issues.

**Upgrading:** Your existing custom groups are still supported. Use the manager to turn individual built-in groups on or off.

**Still being worked on:** Very large groups and resource reloads can still cause noticeable pauses.
