# Collapsible Groups 2.0.0-beta2 — Minecraft 1.20.1

- Cleaner category controls, with a searchable picker for moving groups and more consistent button feedback.
- Hide the category sidebar or turn off built-in groups by category in settings.
- Added GTCEu Modern groups and corrected Macaw's, Iron's Spells, and Apotheosis groups for 1.20.1.
- Large groups are much quicker to edit. Saving only refreshes the group you changed.
- Fixed a startup crash with newer JEI 15 builds while keeping support for older versions.

Very large groups may still take a few seconds to refresh after saving or reloading resources.
