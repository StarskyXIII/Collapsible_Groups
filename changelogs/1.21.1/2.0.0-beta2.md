# Collapsible Groups 2.0.0-beta2 — Minecraft 1.21.1

- Organize groups into categories, with a collapsible sidebar and a searchable picker for moving groups.
- All loaders now share one settings screen. Hide the category sidebar or turn off built-in groups by category.
- Added GTCEu Modern groups and updated Macaw's and Iron's Spells groups.
- Large groups are much quicker to edit. Saving only refreshes the group you changed.
- Fixed button press feedback, disabled buttons, and several manager layout issues.
- Fixed KubeJS group integration across older and newer KubeJS builds on NeoForge.

Very large groups may still take a few seconds to refresh after saving or reloading resources.
